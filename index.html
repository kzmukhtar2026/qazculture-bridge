About QazCulture Bridge

Project “Can AI Translate Kazakh Culture?” addresses a critical gap in modern multilingual machine learning models: the loss of intangible cultural heritage during literal translation.

What is KÖPIR әдісі?

KÖPIR (meaning "bridge" in Kazakh) is a specialized translation framework that combines phonological preservation of unique national terms with deep semantic context notes, preventing cultural erosion in AI outputs.

The prototype utilizes a verified database of 25 core cultural terms to demonstrate how high accuracy scores (up to 8/8) can be achieved across Meaning, Culture, Literary Value, and Clarity.
